Photosynthesis in action

Chlorophyll is the plant’s light catcher

CO₂ entersH₂O risesO₂ leaveschlorophyll captures light

Green chlorophyll in the leaves absorbs sunlight. The plant takes in carbon dioxide and water, builds sugar for growth, and releases oxygen back into the air.
